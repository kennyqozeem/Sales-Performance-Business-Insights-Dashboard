# 📊 Sales Performance & Business Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales performance data to uncover meaningful business insights across revenue, profitability, product categories, geographical regions, sales channels, and product returns.

Using Microsoft Excel, I organised and analysed sales data to evaluate business performance, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project demonstrates how data analytics can transform raw transactional data into actionable business intelligence for sales managers, commercial teams, and business executives.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyse overall sales revenue, profit, and profit margins.
* Evaluate sales performance across different states.
* Identify product categories contributing to revenue and profitability.
* Compare sales performance across Online, In-store, and WhatsApp channels.
* Examine monthly revenue trends.
* Analyse product return rates across sales channels.
* Present key business metrics in a format that supports informed decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Microsoft Excel** — Data cleaning, transformation, analysis, PivotTables, KPI calculations, and visualisation.
* **PivotTables** — Aggregating and summarising sales data across business dimensions.
* **Excel Charts** — Communicating performance trends and comparisons visually.
* **Data Analytics** — Identifying patterns, evaluating business performance, and generating insights.

## 📂 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains sales transaction records with information covering:

* Transaction ID
* Transaction date
* Store ID and geographical state
* Sales channel
* Product category and product name
* Customer ID and customer type
* Units sold and unit price
* Discount percentage
* Revenue and cost
* Profit
* Return status
* Sales representative
* Month

The workbook includes raw data, cleaned data, pivot-based summaries, and a dashboard worksheet.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The analysis evaluates the following business metrics:

| KPI                  | Description                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Total Revenue        | Total recorded sales revenue                |
| Total Profit         | Profit generated from sales                 |
| Profit Margin        | Profit as a percentage of revenue           |
| Total Orders         | Number of recorded transactions             |
| Units Sold           | Total quantity of products sold             |
| Return Rate          | Percentage of orders marked as returned     |
| Monthly Revenue      | Revenue performance across months           |
| Regional Revenue     | Sales revenue across geographical locations |
| Category Performance | Revenue and profit by product category      |
| Channel Performance  | Sales and returns across sales channels     |

## 🔍 Key Findings

Based on the workbook's aggregated figures:

* **Total Revenue:** ₦47,810,625
* **Total Profit:** ₦14,970,025
* **Overall Profit Margin:** Approximately 31.3%
* **Total Orders:** 432
* **Returned Orders:** 38
* **Overall Return Rate:** Approximately 8.8%

### Regional Performance

The analysis compares revenue, profit, and profit margins across Lagos, Kano, Ibadan, Port Harcourt, and Abuja.

Lagos recorded the highest revenue among the five locations, at approximately ₦13.91 million.

### Product Category Performance

The analysis covers Electronics, Home, and Accessories.

Electronics generated the largest revenue contribution, at approximately ₦30.04 million. Accessories recorded the highest profit margin among the three categories, at approximately 46.9%.

### Sales Channel Performance

The workbook compares In-store, Online, and WhatsApp sales channels.

| Channel   |  Orders | Returned Orders | Return Rate |
| --------- | ------: | --------------: | ----------: |
| In-store  |     208 |              13 |       6.25% |
| Online    |     137 |              15 |      10.95% |
| WhatsApp  |      87 |              10 |      11.49% |
| **Total** | **432** |          **38** |   **8.80%** |

These results provide a basis for investigating channel-level differences in customer returns and identifying areas where operational improvements may be needed.

## ⚙️ Analytical Process

The project follows a structured data analytics workflow:

1. **Data Inspection:** Reviewed the raw transactional dataset and its structure.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Worked with a cleaned dataset to support consistent analysis.
3. **Data Transformation:** Prepared relevant fields and metrics for reporting.
4. **Data Aggregation:** Used PivotTables to summarise performance by region, product category, sales channel, and month.
5. **KPI Analysis:** Evaluated revenue, profit, margins, order volumes, and return rates.
6. **Visualisation:** Used Excel reporting and charting capabilities to communicate business performance.
7. **Insight Generation:** Identified notable performance patterns and potential areas for further investigation.
